Risk Radar is a lightweight AI workflow that converts messy project updates into delivery signals. The prototype analyzes updates for risks, blockers, dependencies, timeline impact, and recommended actions, then presents them as a project-health dashboard. I used self-created dummy data to avoid confidential information and designed the prompt to return structured JSON, making the output easy to validate and integrate into tools such as Jira or Slack. Each flagged item includes supporting evidence to reduce unsupported conclusions. The prototype separates current blockers from future risks and highlights cross-team dependencies, helping teams escalate issues before they affect delivery timelines and decisions.
